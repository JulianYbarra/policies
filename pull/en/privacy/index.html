Privacy policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

In short: everything you enter in Pull (teams, players, games, tournaments) stays on your phone and never reaches us. There's no account and no ads. To improve the app, Pull uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which receive technical usage and crash data, never your teams or players.

What Pull keeps, and where

Your teams, the players you add (name, number, gender, position and line role), games with their points and events, tournaments, spirit scores and your settings are stored in Pull's database on your phone. We have no servers and never receive them.

Other people's data

If you add players from your team or other teams, you're responsible for having their agreement. That data stays on your phone unless you share it.

.pull files

When you export a team, game or tournament, Pull creates a .pull file with that data and hands it to the app you choose (for example, a chat or mail app). From then on the file is subject to that app's terms and to whoever receives it. When you open a .pull file, Pull reads it on your phone.

Analytics and crash reports

Pull uses Firebase Analytics to understand in general how the app is used (for example, app opens, sessions and installed version) and Firebase Crashlytics to get a report when the app closes because of an error. Google, as the Firebase provider, may receive:

  • app installation identifiers and, on Android, the advertising ID if your device allows it;
  • device model, operating system, language, app version and approximate country or region (from the IP address);
  • automatic usage events and, after a crash, the technical details of the error and the device state at that moment.

This data doesn't include your team or player names or the content of your games. We don't sell it, show ads or use it to profile you. Google handles it under Firebase privacy and the Google privacy policy, and keeps it for a limited time.

On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in Settings, Google, Ads (or Privacy, Ads).

Backups

On Android, Pull is excluded from Google backups. On iPhone, if you back up your device (iCloud or a computer), the system may include Pull's data, under Apple's terms and your settings.

Permissions

Pull doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It uses the internet only to send the analytics and crash data described above. Clock alerts sound and vibrate on your phone.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app and everything Pull stored goes with it. There's no copy anywhere else. If you want us to ask Google to delete the analytics data tied to your installation, write to us.

Children

Pull isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that leaves your phone, such as an account, sync or live games for others, this page will say so before that version is released, with the date above changed.
