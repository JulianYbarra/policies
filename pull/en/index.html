Every point, from pull to score.

Pull tracks ultimate game stats from the sideline as the game is played, no signal needed.

  • Build your roster and call each O or D line in two taps.
  • Run the game clock: pull timer, timeouts, halftime and caps, with WFDF whistle counts.
  • See goals, assists, blocks and turnovers for every player, your team's holds and breaks, stats for the whole tournament and the spirit score of every game.
  • Share teams, games and tournaments as a .pull file.

Free, no ads, no account. Your data stays on your phone.
