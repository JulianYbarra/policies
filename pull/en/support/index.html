Support

Something not working, or an idea? Write to junka.contact@gmail.com and you'll get an answer from the person who makes Pull.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Pull works without one and your data stays on your phone.

Does it work offline? Yes. You can track a whole game from the sideline with no signal.

How do I move my data to another phone or person? Export the team, game or tournament as a .pull file and open it with Pull on the other phone.

What happens if I uninstall the app? Everything Pull stored is deleted. Export what you want to keep first.

How do I delete my data? Uninstall the app. No copy is kept anywhere else (privacy policy).
